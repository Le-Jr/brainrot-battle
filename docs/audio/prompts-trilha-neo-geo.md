# Brainrot Battaglia — prompts para uma nova trilha

Prompts independentes, em inglês, prontos para colar no campo de descrição musical de uma IA. Gere uma faixa por vez. Se houver opção **Instrumental**, ative-a; não cole os prompts no campo de letra.

A direção é **arcade Neo Geo dos anos 90 com produção encorpada**, melodias que você consegue cantar, baixo expressivo, bateria com impacto e desenvolvimento de verdade. Evitar reduzir essa estética a bipes e arpejos genéricos de 8 bits. As referências descrevem uma sonoridade; não é necessário simular fielmente as limitações do hardware.

As durações e os loops abaixo são pedidos musicais: o gerador pode não respeitá-los exatamente. Confira a repetição e edite a emenda antes de usar no jogo. Se a ferramenta gerar uma música completa com final, peça uma versão contínua do trecho de combate.

```text

Punchy sampled rock drums, expressive slap bass, gritty surf electric guitar, bold FM synth brass and a soaring melodic lead. Write a catchy, singable four-bar signature hook, then answer it with guitar and brass call-and-response. Structure: immediate groove, main hook, contrasting syncopated bridge, bigger hook reprise, short drum turnaround returning to the opening. Sunny, cocky, fast and exhilarating; a real beach fight, not relaxing beach music. Target a 75–90 second continuous gameplay loop. Full-bodied arcade production with clear melody and room for combat sound effects. No vocals, speech, generic 8-bit beeps, ambient intro, modern EDM drop or fade-out.
```

## 15 · Mega Vaca FINAL — Toccata della Mega Vacca

```text
Instrumental final-boss battle remix of Johann Sebastian Bach's Toccata and Fugue in D minor, BWV 565, arranged as a spectacular 1990s Neo Geo arcade symphonic-metal soundtrack. 172 BPM, D minor. Preserve and clearly feature the recognizable opening Toccata motif and descending gestures; this must be an audible musical adaptation of BWV 565, not merely an unrelated dark organ track.

Make the organ melody clearly audible above the guitars. Powerful, full-bodied arcade texture with modern clarity, not thin 8-bit chiptune and not an ambient orchestral trailer.

Structure: a short 4–8 second theatrical statement of the famous opening; immediate full-band battle groove; a furious rhythmic variation of the Toccata; an imitative contrapuntal passage drawing on the Fugue subject, with audible musical answers between organ and guitar; a brief ominous breakdown; then an overwhelming final reprise with the Bach motif in octaves, guitar harmonies and relentless drums. A cosmic final confrontation against an absurdly powerful Mega Cow: terrifying, majestic, urgent and ultimately heroic. Treat the fight completely seriously, with no comedy sound effects.

Target 100–120 seconds. After the short introduction, design a sustained combat section that can repeat, ending with a dominant turnaround back into the main battle riff rather than a final cadence. Strong melodic identity, real harmonic movement, dynamic contrast and an epic climax. No vocals, choir, lyrics, speech, cow sounds, modern EDM drop, long ambient opening or fade-out. Compose and perform a fresh arrangement; do not sample an existing recording.
```

### Se a IA ignorar Bach

Use esta instrução de revisão, junto da faixa ou do prompt anterior:

```text
Make BWV 565 substantially more recognizable. Feature Bach's famous opening Toccata phrase clearly in the foreground before introducing new material, and return to it as the main battle hook. Keep its melodic contour and D-minor identity. Reduce unrelated shredding and background pads. Give the organ the lead and use the guitars to answer and harmonize it. Keep the 172 BPM arcade-metal groove and the enormous final-boss energy.
```

Se a ferramenta aceitar referência sonora, uma interpretação própria do motivo em MIDI/áudio pode ajudar a fixar a melodia. Só texto pode produzir uma aproximação em vez de um remix fiel.

## 17 · Continue? — O Último Segundo

Tela após a derrota com contagem de 9 a 0 (`cont`). O jogador pode acelerar a contagem ou interrompê-la ao continuar: os números e o anúncio de GAME OVER ficam por conta do jogo.

```text
Instrumental continue-screen theme for a 1990s Neo Geo arcade fighting game. 120 BPM, A minor, tense and dramatic with one last spark of hope. Begin immediately with a distinctive descending electric-piano melody over a restrained pulsing synth bass, tight sampled kick, dry snare, metallic FM accents and short dark brass responses. A defeated fighter waits beside a large countdown: make the player feel the urgency to press Start and fight again. Compose a compact four-bar phrase, approximately eight seconds, with a clear melodic identity and a harmonically unresolved turnaround that loops naturally into the first beat. Keep the tension steady across repetitions; the game can accelerate or interrupt its countdown independently. Full-bodied early-1990s arcade production, clear midrange and space for separately played countdown announcements and interface effects. No vocals, spoken numbers, ticking-clock samples, game-over announcement, long intro, thin 8-bit arpeggios, EDM drop, final cadence or fade-out. Deliver a repeatable music bed, not a fixed countdown or a pre-timed game-over sequence.
```

## Ajustes rápidos para qualquer faixa

**Ficou genérica ou sem melodia:**

```text
Rewrite around a distinctive, singable four-bar main melody. Repeat it clearly, answer it with a second phrase, develop it in the bridge, and bring it back in a stronger arrangement. Fewer random notes and fewer generic arpeggios. Make this sound like a memorable fighting-game stage theme.
```

**Ficou fina ou com cara de brinquedo:**

```text
Use a full-bodied 1990s arcade band: expressive electric bass, punchy sampled rock drums, bold FM brass, convincing guitar or keyboard leads, and strong midrange presence. Replace tiny square-wave beeps and generic 8-bit arpeggios. Keep the retro arcade character while making the instruments substantial and exciting.
```

**Ficou parada:**

```text
Establish the battle groove within the first two seconds. Add rhythmic bass movement, stronger drum accents, short fills every four or eight bars, melodic call-and-response, and a contrasting bridge. Increase urgency without turning the track into a modern EDM drop.
```

**Precisa virar loop:**

```text
Create a gameplay-loop version of the main combat section. Remove the intro, final cadence and fade-out. Keep a stable tempo and an exact whole number of musical bars. End with a short turnaround that resolves naturally into the opening beat, preserving the reverb and delay tails across the loop boundary.
```
